# Sources and use conditions

[Inventory](inventory.csv) records each dataset's source, inclusion basis,
attribution and use conditions. Shared L5 files serve all three chains, with one
physical copy. Each input inherits the conditions of the dataset it supports.

| Source | Recorded basis and attribution |
|---|---|
| Binance Vision | Credit Binance Vision; CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 and the recorded Vision terms; disclose consolidation and timestamp conversion |
| Coin Metrics | Credit Coin Metrics; CC BY-NC 4.0 and inherited third-party terms; retain distinct capitalization definitions |
| CoinSpectrum | Credit CoinSpectrum; CC BY 4.0; retain source and archive times |
| growthepie | Credit growthepie; CC BY 4.0; identify the selected dates and metrics |
| BLS and U.S. Treasury | Government-produced statistical material; cite the agency, series, snapshot and extraction |
| New York Fed | Retain the prescribed EFFR notice below and the applicable source terms |
| Raydium and CoinMarketCap | Included under the recorded project decisions, with the source-specific permission evidence preserved in the inventory |
| Authored facts and RPC calculations | Retain source citations, researcher transformations and measurement scope |

CMC attribution: Source: CoinMarketCap (https://coinmarketcap.com).
Source-specific project decisions and permission evidence are recorded together in
the inventory and accompany the selected data.
